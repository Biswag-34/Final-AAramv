Property FAQs exact copy
Which homes are currently available? Availability depends on the selected phase, layout and transaction type. Ask us for the current options for the home you are considering.
Is the displayed price the complete purchase cost? The displayed amount may be a base price or asking price. Request the current cost sheet for the selected home, including applicable taxes, registration and other charges.
Which area measurement is shown? Read the area label beside the figure. Carpet, built-up, super built-up and plot areas are different measures; compare the same basis across homes.
Does the project status apply to every home? Different phases, blocks or homes can have different completion and possession information. Review the documents relevant to the option you select.
Can I visit before booking? You can request a site visit. Access and timing need to be agreed separately with the project or seller.
What should I review before EOI or booking? Ask for the relevant cost sheet, payment schedule, written EOI or booking terms and the documents for the selected home. Review cancellation and refund conditions before paying.
Does Aaramv guarantee legal clearance or loan approval? No. Aaramv helps coordinate information and questions. Legal review, lending decisions and transaction commitments remain with the relevant professionals and parties.
What happens after booking? Aaramv continues to help coordinate project updates, document requests and possession-related next steps through move-in, within the support scope agreed for your transaction.
